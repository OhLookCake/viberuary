01 / The guidelines

A very loose
definition of rules.

  1. 01

    Make a thing.

    Use AI to help you code something each day in February. Big project or small experiment; website, game, software, anything. You bring the idea; the The machine brings its characteristic confidence.

  2. 02

    Follow a prompt. Or don't.

    Each day has a suggested theme below. Use it as a starting point, interpret it very loosely, or wander off entirely. It's for inspiration, not homework.

  3. 03

    Show your working.

    Share a link, a screenshot, a video, or a small tale of what went wrong. Use #viberuary so others can find it. Showing a friend also counts. Your friend has been warned.

  4. 04

    Do it again.

    Aim for all 28 days. Every other day or once a week is welcome too. Miss a day? Carry on. This is a creative habit, not a court summons.

03 / The FAQs

What can I make?

Create a web page. Or an app. Or a tool. Or a game.
Or an experiment that's just fun. Or cool. Or silly.
Or anything else you can imagine and persuade a computer to do.

Which tools should I use?

Whatever you like. Mix LLMs, languages, frameworks, manual edits...

Does it have to be a new project every day?

No. Make a tiny standalone thing, or add a feature to a bigger project, or remix something you made earlier. Just make some progress that day. Renaming a file is technically progress, but you can probably do better.

Where do I post? Is there a prize?

Post wherever you already share things, with #viberuary if you'd like to be found. A private demo to a friend counts too. There are no submissions or prizes. This is just a collection of things that didn't exist in January :)

Isn't this just promoting slop?

Pre-judging AI created artifacts as slop is a silly take. It can be slop, but it can also be beautiful. Take the reins

I found this in the middle of February, what do I do?

Starting in the middle of the month is totally fine. If you want to go back to the prompts you missed later on, have at it
