# Vibember

**A month of vibe coding. Real creativity. Artificial intelligence.**

Vibember is a creative challenge to make something with AI every day of November. Thirty days to try ideas, learn something, and bring things into the world that didn't exist in October.

Make a website, an app, a game, a useful tool, or a strange little experiment. Whether you use AI coding tools every day or you're just getting started, you're welcome to join.

Visit [vibember.pages.dev](https://vibember.pages.dev) for the guidelines, daily prompts, and FAQs.

## How to take part

1. **Make something.** Use AI to help you code each day from November 1–30. Choose whichever tools, languages, and frameworks you like.
2. **Try a prompt, if you want.** There are 30 optional prompts, one for each day. Take them literally, interpret them loosely, or follow your own idea.
3. **Share what you make.** Post a link, screenshot, video, or story about the process wherever you already share things. Use **#vibember** so others can find your work. A private demo to a friend counts too.
4. **Keep going at your own pace.** Aim for all 30 days, join once a week, or start late. Miss a day? Carry on.

You can start a new project each day, add a feature to something bigger, or remix an earlier experiment. Unfinished projects count. The point is to make progress and flex your creative muscles.

There are no formal submissions or prizes. Pick an idea and start making.
